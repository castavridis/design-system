Ramps

Every brand colour expands into an eleven-step tonal scale — 50 through 950 — including the two neutrals. Steps are placed by perceived lightness rather than mixed toward white and black, so purple-300 and teal-300 read as the same brightness. The ringed step is the brand colour itself, kept exactly, at whichever step it perceptually belongs to.

Generated by rampStops() in src/pmndrs-design-book.ts from the brand colours alone — nothing here is hand-picked. Add a colour to the brand scope and its ramp appears on the next build.

Gradient generator

Three stops at fixed positions — 16%, 50% and 84%. Any brand colour is available except dark and light, and no two adjacent stops may be the same, so the options matching a neighbour are disabled below rather than silently rejected.

References stay live

brand.radius is defined as ref('brand.space'), and the CSS renderer preserves that as --brand-radius: var(--brand-space). Change the one input and everything downstream follows — no rebuild.

Gha

GitHub-style alerts. Five keywords, each mapped to one accent ramp — tint from 950, rule and icon from the brand step.

Highlights information that users should take into account, even when skimming. Optional information to help a user be more successful. Crucial information necessary for users to succeed. Critical content demanding immediate user attention due to potential risks. Negative potential consequences of an action.

Gha[title]

A title prop replaces the label; the icon and tint still follow the keyword.
